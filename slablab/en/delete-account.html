Delete your account

Last updated: October 2026

Slab Lab, published by Vergasta Digital, does not ask you to create an account. For the online save, the game creates an anonymous account, which you can link to Google or Apple. This page explains how to delete that account and the data linked to it.

In the game

  1. Open Slab Lab and tap the gear, top right.
  2. In the “Cloud save” section, tap “Delete my account”.
  3. Confirm. If your account is linked to Google or Apple, the system asks you to sign in one last time.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. The online save is then turned off; you can turn it back on later, with a new account.

What is deleted

  • The online copy of your lab.
  • The game account and its link with Google or Apple: the account identifier, the email address and sign-in dates.
  • With Apple, the authorisation given to Slab Lab is also revoked.

What stays

  • The lab saved on your device: it disappears when you delete the app.
  • Purchase information, kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for as long as the law requires, in particular for accounting.
  • TelemetryDeck’s anonymous usage analytics and Google AdMob’s advertising data, which are not linked to your account. See the privacy policy.

Without access to the game

If you no longer have the game, write to us through the Vergasta Digital contact form, with the email address of the Google or Apple account linked to your save. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm it by email.

A save never linked to Google or Apple is tied to no information that could identify you: it can only be deleted from the game, on the device that created it. Without an update for 18 months, it is erased automatically anyway.

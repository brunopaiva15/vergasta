Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers the game Slab Lab for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by Vergasta Digital. Slab Lab is played without an account: the game does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and it contains no forced advertising: the only ad is an optional video you choose to watch.

Data processing is governed in particular by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where applicable, by the GDPR. Information about the publisher is available in the Vergasta Digital legal notice.

Slab Lab is in development and currently distributed to testers. This policy describes the current version. It will be updated before any feature that would process other data is added, such as an online save or leaderboards.

Your lab stays on your device

Your game (money, holos, cards, slabs, staff, upgrades, cosmetics, settings) is saved locally, in the app’s storage. A snapshot of the current lot and its timers is shared, on the same device, with the widgets, the Live Activity and the tracking notification. This data is not sent to Vergasta Digital.

Depending on your device settings, it may be included in system backups (iCloud Backup, Google backup). It is then processed by Apple or Google under their own terms, and Vergasta Digital has no access to it. Deleting the app deletes the game from the device.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn how the game is played and improve it, Slab Lab sends usage signals to the TelemetryDeck service. A signal describes a step in the game, for example starting a session, a tutorial step, a completed goal, a graded lot, opening the shop, a purchase (the product bought, without any amount or payment data) or tapping the share button. It carries the platform and the game’s language.

The service adds technical information such as the game and system versions, the device model, the language and region settings, and a hashed pseudonymous identifier that cannot be traced back to you. TelemetryDeck uses no advertising identifier. This data is used only for aggregate statistics.

In-app purchases

Slab Lab is free and offers in-app purchases and a subscription, Lab Pro. Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Vergasta Digital does not receive your card details, your name or the email address of your store account.

Purchases go through the RevenueCat service, which verifies transactions with the store and lets you restore your purchases. To do so, RevenueCat processes an anonymous identifier created by the game, the transaction information provided by the store (product, price, currency, date, store country, subscription status, transaction identifiers) and technical connection data such as the IP address, device model and system version.

Optional rewarded video

After you have been away, the game may offer to double the money you earned by watching a short video ad. It is never forced, and never appears in the first minutes of play, on the lock screen or in notifications. Based on a draw made at install, some players never see it: this lets us compare the two versions of the game.

The video is provided by Google AdMob. When the game prepares and shows it, Google processes data about the device and the connection (IP address, model, system, language, the Android advertising ID or an app-specific identifier on iPhone), data about the ad shown and your interactions with it, and your consent choices. Google uses this data under its own rules.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent the first time you choose the video, and ads are only personalised if you accept. You can change your mind at any time in the game’s settings, with “Ad privacy choices”. On iPhone, Slab Lab does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency): the device’s advertising identifier is not used. On Android, you can delete or reset the advertising ID in the system settings.

Sharing a slab

The share button creates an image of the slab you got and a short line of text on the device, then opens the system share sheet. Nothing is sent until you pick a destination. The shared content is then handled by the app or service you chose.

Notifications, Live Activity and widgets

While a lot is being graded, Slab Lab can show its countdown in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island on iPhone, or in a tracking notification on Android, as well as in widgets. They are produced on the device, without a notification server: the game does not register any remote notification token.

On Android 13 and later, the game asks for permission to show notifications. On iPhone, Live Activities are managed in the system settings. A game setting turns them off; Slab Lab remains playable without them.

Test versions

If you test Slab Lab through TestFlight or a Google Play test, Apple or Google share with Vergasta Digital the information provided for by these programmes, such as the email address or name used for the invitation, crash reports, and the feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This information is used only to fix and improve the game.

Main service providers

  • Apple: App Store, TestFlight, payment for purchases and system services (Live Activity, widgets, iCloud Backup).
  • Google: Google Play, closed testing, payment for purchases and system services (notifications, widgets, Google backup).
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States): verification and restoration of purchases and the subscription.
  • TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany): anonymous usage analytics.
  • Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, and Google LLC, United States): the optional rewarded video and the ad consent message.

These providers may also process the ordinary technical data of an Internet connection, such as the IP address, under their own policies and legal obligations. Some process data outside Switzerland, in particular in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by law, such as standard contractual clauses or Data Privacy Framework certification where it applies.

Retention and deletion

The game saved on your device stays under your control and disappears with the app or its data. Usage signals and purchase information are kept as long as they are needed for the game’s statistics, for managing purchases and for legal obligations, according to the retention periods of the providers concerned.

Children

Slab Lab does not ask for players’ age and does not collect any data that identifies them. In-app purchases by a minor require the consent of their parents or guardians. Apple and Google offer parental controls to allow or block purchases, such as Ask to Buy or Family Link.

Security

Exchanges with providers use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The game does not request access to your location, camera, photo library, microphone or contacts.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may in particular request access to, correction or erasure of personal data, restrict or object to certain processing and, where the law provides for it, data portability. Since Slab Lab does not know your name or address, we generally cannot link any data to you: for a request about a purchase, give us the order ID shown on the store receipt. Write to us through the Vergasta Digital contact form.

Changes and complaints

This policy may change as Slab Lab’s features or the services it uses evolve. The date of the current version is shown at the top of the page.

In Switzerland, a complaint may be lodged with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. In the European Union, you may contact the data protection authority of your country.

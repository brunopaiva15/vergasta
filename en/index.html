What we do

Presentation websites
Showing a company, what it offers and how to reach it without the visitor having to hunt for any of it, and helping it turn up when someone searches for its trade. That is what we built for F. Da Silva sàrl, a tiling company in Péry-La Heutte.
Applications
When the binder or the Excel file no longer holds, we build the tool that takes over. Disque Bleu works out the time to set on a parking disc and gives warning before it runs out. Axolot simplifies invoicing and bookkeeping for small Swiss companies. Both started from the same observation: a repetitive task was costing time.
Online shops
Catalogue, basket, card or TWINT payment through Stripe, order tracking and an administration interface you run yourself. Custom To Lylia and BDPokéCards work on this basis.
Creative projects
Some projects start from an idea someone wants to make tangible. Yamanote 3D recreates a quiet ride on the Yamanote line in Tokyo, inside the browser. Vergasta Photo shows photographic collections in a portfolio built for the purpose, with its own publishing studio.

How a project runs

It starts with a conversation, by phone or over a coffee in St-Imier, to understand the business and what the site really has to do. You then receive a detailed quote by email, with the price and the schedule.

A mock-up is approved before the first line of code. During development, a test address lets you look at where the work stands whenever you want, without waiting for a presentation.

At launch, we take care of the domain, the redirects and the indexing settings. The source code is yours as soon as the invoice is paid, and you stay free to hand it to someone else afterwards.

Worth knowing

  • Fixed quote, deposit on order.
  • Fixing the bugs present at delivery is included in the price.
  • Hosting and domain name are at your expense, unless agreed otherwise.
  • The Vergasta Digital credit stays in the footer of the delivered site.
  • A maintenance contract is available once the warranty has run out.

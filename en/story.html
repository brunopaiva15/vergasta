Our story

A workshop looks like the person who runs it. Here is where this one comes from, told by Bruno Vergasta Paiva, from the first computer sitting on a bedroom desk to the sign on the rue du Temple.

  1. 2002

    Where it starts

    My name is Bruno, I am 23, and I live in Switzerland. The Bernese Jura is where I grew up and where I work today. Distances are short here, you know the people you work for, and a website you deliver always ends up in the shop window next door.

  2. 2011

    The first computer

    I was nine when a computer arrived at home, and it was mine. I think I was born into computing: what could have stayed a toy became, straight away, a place to build things. From that day on I never had the slightest doubt about the field I would work in.

  3. Then

    Development

    Using the machine did not satisfy me for very long. I wanted to know what was behind the window: how a button knows it has been clicked, how a page makes it all the way to a screen. The first time a program written by my own hand did exactly what I asked of it, the passion was there. It has never left.

  4. Since

    The art of things done properly

    Over the projects, a second passion settled in, harder to name in English. Japanese has a word for it.

    This way of seeing things comes in part from a trip to Japan. The same care shows up there in trades that have nothing to do with mine, and it has not left me since.

    A pixel put in line, half a second saved on a page load: none of it shows up on a quote, and yet all of it is felt in use, years after delivery.

  5. Today

    Vergasta Digital

    That is what I try to carry into every piece of work I deliver, from online shops to applications, right down to the page you are reading. It matters a great deal to me.

    And that is Vergasta Digital.

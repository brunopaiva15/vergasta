Experiences

Each of the workshop’s projects has a moment where it moves. We have taken one per project, starting from its own code, so that you can watch it and handle it here, without installing the app or opening the site.

  • Yamanote 3D

    The screen above the doors of the E235 trains, which the game paints exactly as it is: next stations, map of the loop, exits and notices, in Japanese then in English. The train is simulated here, three times faster than in reality and on Tokyo time.

    yamanote-3d.com

  • Disque Bleu

    The app’s disc, redrawn line for line. Turn it with a finger or the mouse: flick it and it keeps its momentum. When you let go, it shows the time to set, always the next half hour, as the app works it out for Switzerland. The orange area is the permitted parking time.

    disquebleusuisse.ch

  • BDPokéCards

    On a card’s page, it tilts towards the pointer and catches the light, with a rainbow sheen. Here, the Pikachu 173/165 from the 151 set, as the shop presents it.

    bd-pokecards.ch

    Stellar Rebirth

    The seven tableaux of the novel’s prologue, drawn in line as in its reading app. Each one draws itself, then gives way to the next; tap the scene to turn the page.

    stellar-rebirth.com

  • Drag to turn · Click to enlarge

    Vergasta Photo

    The sphere view of the portfolio’s collections, here the one from Japan. Drag to turn it, and tap a photo to bring it to the centre with its camera settings.

    vergastaphoto.ch

  • Axolot

    While something loads, Axolot shows a corrupted video decode, cut out in the silhouette of its axolotl. Tap the badge to start another one.

    axolot.ch

  • Ta personnalité :) ton équilibre

    Custom To Lylia

    On the shop’s home page, the logo follows the cursor with its gaze, wherever it is on the page. On a touch screen, it breathes.

    customtolylia.ch

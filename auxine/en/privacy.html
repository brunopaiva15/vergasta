Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy applies to the Auxine application, published by Vergasta Digital. Auxine is designed to work primarily on the device and can be used without an account.

Data processing is governed in particular by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where applicable, the GDPR. Information about the publisher is available in the Vergasta Digital legal notice.

Data stored on the device

Without an account, notebook data is stored locally on the device. This may include plants, selected species, rooms and locations, photos, notes, care records, calendars, measurements, inventory, labels, tasks, preferences and other information entered in the application.

Backups and exports created by the user are stored in the location chosen by the user and remain under the user's control.

Iris and local identification

The Iris identification model is built into Auxine. Primary identification is performed directly on the device and can work offline. An identification performed only with Iris does not send the photo to Vergasta Digital.

Optional account and synchronization

On iOS, users may choose to sign in with Apple. An account is not required to keep a local notebook. It can be used to synchronize data, recover gardens and use collaborative features.

When a remote account is used, data from the selected garden may be synchronized through Auxine's Supabase infrastructure. This may include plant records, care records, locations, calendars, measurements, inventory, tags, photos, garden information and technical identifiers required for synchronization.

Shared gardens

In a shared garden, authorized members may view or modify data according to their role. Actions and photos may be associated with their author so that the application can show who made a change.

Invitations use a single-use code and expire by default after fourteen days. An invitation may be restricted to an email address. When an invitation link is shared in a messaging service, its preview may display the garden name and the display name of the inviter. The messaging service may cache this information.

Community tips

Species-related tips can be read without an account. Publishing, voting and reporting require an account. A published tip is visible to other users together with the author information provided by the application. Votes and reports are recorded to prevent duplicates and enable moderation.

Optional contribution to improving Iris

Sending photos to improve Iris is disabled by default. It is enabled only after the user's explicit choice and requires a remote account.

When this option is enabled and an identification is confirmed, Auxine may send up to three reduced-size photos, the selected species, model suggestions and scores, the Iris version, the application version and the account identifier.

Before upload, images are reoriented and reduced, and GPS coordinates contained in EXIF metadata are removed. These items are stored separately from garden photos. The option can be disabled in the identification settings.

Online search with Pl@ntNet

Pl@ntNet search is optional and may complement Iris when online search is enabled. If used, the photos selected for identification are sent to the Pl@ntNet API together with the parameters required for the request. This feature can be disabled in settings.

Photo diagnosis and Infomaniak

Photo diagnosis requires an internet connection. When the user intentionally starts an analysis, the photo and the information required for the request are sent to Infomaniak AI Services to provide diagnostic suggestions. This service is hosted in Switzerland.

Other assistance features may also use these services to complete care information or refine certain content when the user activates the relevant feature.

Weather and location

Auxine may ask for permission to access the device location in order to select local weather. The request uses low accuracy. Coordinates stored by the application are rounded to two decimal places, approximately one kilometre. The city name is determined using the system geocoding service.

To obtain forecasts and climate information, the coordinates of the selected location are sent to Open-Meteo. When a city is searched manually, the search text is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service.

Device permissions

Auxine may request access to the camera, photo library, notifications and, when the feature is used, compatible Apple Home data. These permissions are used only for the requested features, for example taking a plant photo, attaching an image, scanning a label, scheduling a reminder or reading a temperature or humidity sensor.

Optional support purchase

Auxine is free and has no subscription. A one-time optional purchase can support development without unlocking any feature. On iOS, the purchase is processed by the App Store. Vergasta Digital does not receive the bank card details used by Apple for payment.

Main service providers

  • Apple: TestFlight, App Store, Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and system services used with the user's permission.
  • Supabase: authentication, optional synchronization, storage of synchronized photos, shared gardens, community features and Iris feedback when these features are enabled.
  • Infomaniak: processing of assistance and photo diagnosis requests.
  • Pl@ntNet: optional online identification.
  • Open-Meteo: location search, weather and climate data.

These providers may also process ordinary technical connection data, such as IP addresses, according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Retention and deletion

Data stored only on the device remains under the user's control and can be deleted with the application's local data. Synchronized data is retained for as long as necessary for the account, shared garden or relevant feature. Content deleted in Auxine is deleted or marked for deletion as part of synchronization.

Security

Technical and organizational measures are applied, including HTTPS/TLS, access controls, per-user and per-garden access rules for synchronization, and separation of storage areas used by certain features.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of or objection to certain processing and, where provided by law, data portability. For a request that cannot be completed directly in the application, use the Vergasta Digital contact form.

Changes and complaints

This policy may change as Auxine's features or the services it uses evolve. The date of the current version is shown at the top of the page.

In Switzerland, complaints may be submitted to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

The essentials

Identify, care, watch them grow.

01 · Identification

Iris identifies your plants, even offline.

One photo is enough. A second photo of the same plant refines the result, and both are analyzed together. Suggested species are ranked by probability, and you confirm the species before adding it to the plant record.

On-device analysis
The model is included with the app. Offline identification runs directly on the phone.
Optional online fallback
When Iris is unsure, the app can send the photo to Pl@ntNet. You can turn this fallback off in the settings, and everything then stays on your device.

02 · Care

Every morning, today’s care.

Watering and other scheduled care in one place, with reminders at the time you choose.

The calendar shows upcoming care in agenda or monthly view.

03 · Tracking

Each plant’s journal, in photos.

Care history, notes and growth measurements. Compare two photos before and after, follow growth on a curve and play your plant photos as a timelapse.

The joy of watching them grow.

A new leaf on the pilea, the first hoya flower, basil coming back in spring. Auxine keeps these moments in each plant’s journal, so you can play them back as a timelapse.

And more

Tools for your garden.

Reference, organization, sharing and backup.

Photo diagnosis.

Symptoms, likely causes and corrective steps, ranked by probability. Suggestions to verify.

Requires a connection. Images are analyzed by Infomaniak in Switzerland.

Places, tasks and inventory.

Rooms and outdoor spaces, tasks and events for planting and repotting, and an inventory of pots, soil mixes and fertilizers.

Care guides.

Seasonal watering, light, humidity, fertilizer and repotting. For each species: substrate, propagation and toxicity.

Encyclopedia
Species guides, disorders, pests and diseases, search and a cultivation glossary.
Propagation guides
Illustrated and animated steps for cuttings, division and offsets, with methods adapted to each species. A cutting becomes a new plant, linked to its parent.
Plant finder
Filter by light, location, care needs and toxicity. Outdoor suggestions can also use the local climate.
Weather and Apple Home
Forecasts for the selected city and condition-based advice. Compatible Apple Home sensors provide room temperature and humidity.
Home scan
On iPhone Pro and iPad Pro, LiDAR maps the walls, windows and doors of a room, then the app works out how much light each spot gets, so you know where to put your plants. Everything stays on your device.
Inventory and organization
Pots, soil mixes, fertilizers and equipment, plus tags, filters, custom fields and attachments.
QR labels
Scan codes to open a plant record and create printable PDF label sheets.
Shared gardens
With an account, sync and invite other people to manage a garden together.
Community tips
Read species-specific tips without an account, publish with an account and vote for useful advice.
iOS widgets and shortcuts
Today’s care on the Home Screen and Lock Screen. A long press on the icon lets you add a plant, scan a label or find a new one.
Backup and restore
Export everything or selected categories, including data and photos, then import backups back into Auxine.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does Auxine work offline?

Yes. The journal, care, reminders and identification with Iris work offline: the model is included with the app. Photo diagnosis, the optional Pl@ntNet search, weather, community tips and sync need a connection.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy Auxine once on the App Store. Every feature is included, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Your data is stored on your device. An account is only needed for synchronization, shared gardens and publishing community tips.

How do I identify a plant with Auxine?

Take a photo of the plant, and add a second one if needed. Iris analyzes them together on your device and suggests several species, ranked by probability. You confirm the species before adding it to the plant record.

Where are diagnosis photos analyzed?

Photo diagnosis requires a connection. Images are analyzed by Infomaniak in Switzerland. They are only sent when you start an analysis, and are not kept.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Auxine runs on iPhone and iPad, in French, German, English and Italian. The beta is open on TestFlight.

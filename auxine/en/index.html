Collection, care and calendar.

Collection and locations.

One page per plant, with its species, photos and care history. Organize plants by room or outdoor location.

Today’s care.

Watering and other scheduled care in one place, with reminders at the time you choose.

Calendar and tasks.

Upcoming care in agenda or monthly view, plus custom tasks and events for planting, repotting and garden work.

Locations and inventory keep rooms, outdoor spaces, pots, soil mixes and fertilizers organized.

Identification

Iris, built into Auxine.

Identify plants from photos directly on your device, even without an internet connection.

One or several photos.

Photos of the same plant are analyzed together. Suggested species appear with confidence scores, and you confirm the species before adding it to the plant record.

On-device analysis
The model is included with the app. Offline identification runs directly on the phone.
Optional online search
If the local result is uncertain, Pl@ntNet can complement Iris. Photos are only sent when online search is enabled.

Photo diagnosis

Symptoms, likely causes and care.

A photo and a description of the symptoms provide ranked possibilities, signs to check and corrective steps.

The diagnosis uses a catalog of disorders, pests and diseases. Results remain suggestions to verify.

This feature requires an internet connection. Images are analyzed by Infomaniak in Switzerland.

Journal and photos.

Care history, notes and growth measurements. Compare two photos side by side, view charts and play your plant photos as a timelapse.

Care guides.

Seasonal watering, light, humidity, temperature, fertilizer and repotting. Species guides also cover substrate, propagation and toxicity.

Tools for your garden.

Reference, organization, sharing and backup.

Encyclopedia
Species guides, disorders, pests and diseases, search and a cultivation glossary.
Propagation guides
Illustrated and animated steps for cuttings, division and offsets, with methods adapted to each species.
Plant finder
Filter by light, location, care needs and toxicity. Outdoor suggestions can also use the local climate.
Weather and Apple Home
Forecasts for the selected city and condition-based advice. Compatible Apple Home sensors provide room temperature and humidity.
Inventory and organization
Pots, soil mixes, fertilizers and equipment, plus tags, filters, custom fields and attachments.
QR labels
Scan codes to open a plant record and create printable PDF label sheets.
Shared gardens
With an account, sync and invite other people to manage a garden together.
Community tips
Read species-specific tips without an account, publish with an account and vote for useful advice.
iOS widgets and shortcuts
Today’s care on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, with quick access to add, scan and search.
Backup and restore
Export everything or selected categories, including data and photos, then import backups back into Auxine.
